Frequently asked questions

What counts as a "site"?

Each WordPress install where the plugin is active. A 5-site licence activates on up to five; Agency is unlimited.

What happens when I hit 25 pages on Free?

Nothing breaks. Premium features switch off until you upgrade — and we'll show a one-click 25%-off offer right in the dashboard.

Annual vs lifetime?

Annual includes updates & support while active. Lifetime is a single payment with updates forever, capped at your site count.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes — Free → Pro → Expert, or 1 → 5 → Agency. You only pay the prorated difference.

Refunds?

14 days, no questions asked.

What support is included?

Free — community forum & documentation. Pro — email support. Expert — priority email support with a 24-hour response (business days). Have your licence key handy so we can find your account fast.

Does it replace Yoast or Rank Math?

No — it works alongside them. Keep your SEO plugin for titles, meta and sitemaps; SEO Sprinkler does the page-by-page work they don't.

Does the AI feature send my content to you?

No. AI uses your own OpenAI-compatible endpoint and key. Nothing is proxied through us.

Taxes?

Listed prices exclude VAT/sales tax; it's added at checkout and remitted for you by our payment partner.
