Features

Thirteen on-page tools in one plugin. Free tier in green, automation in Pro, migration & syndication in Expert.

🟢 Free — the hands-on checking tools

Image-minimum audit

Counts inline images, gallery blocks and the featured image; flags articles below your minimum, with a sortable posts-list column.

Schema audit

Fetches rendered pages and detects every Schema.org type; flags missing or required types.

Link audit + inline editor

Internal vs external counts per post, plus a mini-WYSIWYG to fix a link's text/URL in place.

Content cleaner (scan & revert)

Find generative-junk markup; clean it with a per-post backup and one-click revert.

Duplicate-H1 warning

Warns when content includes an <h1> that duplicates the theme title. Dismissible per post.

Business profile + SEO score

A guided questionnaire, "fill with images", and a live 0–100 per-post SEO score on every editor.

🔵 Pro — automation & AI

Automatic internal linking

Display-time (non-destructive) or permanent bulk apply/revert, with whole-word and per-article caps.

Content distribution ("Sprinkler")

Inject shortcodes, images or HTML into matched articles, placed exactly where you want — without touching stored content.

Bulk & auto cleaning

Clean every article at once, or auto-clean on every save.

JSON-LD @graph output

Full structured-data graph in <head> for pages, services, posts and archives.

Auto inbound links on publish

New posts get inbound links from older related articles automatically.

AI meta & titles

Bring your own OpenAI-compatible endpoint and key.

🟣 Expert — scale & move

Export / migrate

Whole-site JSON (post types, taxonomies, media + alt/caption, settings, SEO meta) plus an optional media ZIP.

Syndication

Auto-push each new post to webhooks → Google Business Profile, Facebook, LinkedIn, X (via Zapier/Make/n8n).
